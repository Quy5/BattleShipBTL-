TACTICAL DIRECTIVE

STRATEGIC COMMAND

STATUS CLASSIFIED

HUNT MODE

Checkerboard Grid

Don't fire randomly. Aim for squares separated by one (like the black squares on a chessboard). This helps reduce the number of shots needed to locate any ship that is two or more spaces long.

TARGET MODE

After a "Touchdown" Hit

Immediately after a target is hit, switch to target mode: fire at the 4 adjacent squares (North, South, East, West) until the orientation is determined, then finish off the remaining points.

PROBABILITY

Area Analysis

Focus attacks on open areas where the largest remaining enemy ships could still fit. Ignore small, isolated spaces in the early stages of the battle.

OPTIMAL POSITIONING

Don't place your ships too close together. Avoid predictable corners. Combine horizontal and vertical orientations. Break symmetry to make it harder for the opponent to predict.

LOGGING

Memory isn't always accurate. Use a system to mark every shot (white and red). Identify ships that have been sunk to deduce the scale of remaining threats (e.g., is the 3-square submarine still there?).
